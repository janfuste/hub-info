Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Hub is a personal dashboard used only by its author, on a computer he owns. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google data

With the author's own consent, Hub reads his Google Calendar events, creates events he asks it to create, and reads his Gmail messages (read-only) to show unread counts, senders and subjects.

  • The data is stored only on the author's own computer, in a local database used as a cache.
  • It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or to train models.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, and the local data can be deleted by removing the cache.

Hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
